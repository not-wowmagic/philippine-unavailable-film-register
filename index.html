Status definitions

banned

regulator or court blocked exhibition. Needs a quotable decision, or a report quoting one.

withdrawn

accepted or scheduled, then pulled before public screening. Needs a dated report.

unreleased

finished or partly finished, never publicly exhibited.

unavailable

screened at least once publicly, but no current legal way to watch it. Needs a documented screening and an empty availability search.

lost

no known copy in any archive, studio or private collection. Needs an archive or historian statement, not "I can't find it".

found / partially_found

a copy has been located. Record where and when.

Cause, for unavailable records

never_distributed | distribution_ended | rights_unclear | archive_only | no_known_rights_holder

Confidence

Confirmed means a primary source, archive or regulator. Reported means news. Unverified means a forum or rumour.

The lost rule

Never use "lost" for a film that merely has no streaming link. That is the most common error and it destroys credibility. "Unavailable" is the honest status for a film that screened but has no legal access.

About this register

The Unseen is an independent reference work. It is not affiliated with any studio, archive, festival or regulator.

It covers Philippine films, especially shorts and festival titles, with no current legal access. A few resolved cases stay because the register tracks how films come back, through a print found in an archive or a legal edition released years after a ban.

Each record starts with a documented screening, production or regulatory fact. Availability is checked against legal streaming, VOD, home video and archive catalogues, and the check date is recorded.

The 8,000+ figure is an archive estimate for films shot on film stock from 1919 to 2012. It is not a title list. The hard-to-find index starts from 2,034 Wikidata titles, screened against JustWatch Philippines on 1 November 2026. The corpus estimate comes from The Philippine Star, 17 August 2026, citing Leo Katigbak of ABS-CBN Film Archives.

This is desk research, not an archive visit. Catalogues and region locks change, so a negative finding is not proof that no copy exists. No unauthorized full-film links are included. Corrections should add a source and update the changelog.
