Status definitions

banned

a regulator or court blocked exhibition. It needs a quotable decision, or a report quoting one.

withdrawn

accepted or scheduled, then pulled before public screening. It needs a dated report.

unreleased

finished or partly finished, never publicly exhibited.

unavailable

screened at least once publicly, but no current legal way to watch it. It needs a documented screening and an availability search that found nothing.

lost

no known copy in any archive, studio or private collection. It needs an archive or historian statement, not "I can't find it".

found / partially_found

a copy has been located. Record where and when.

Confidence

Confirmed means a primary source, archive or regulator. Reported means news reporting, festival pages or film databases. Unverified means a forum, rumour or community database.

The lost rule

Do not use "lost" for a film that only has no streaming link. "Lost" means no archive, studio or private collection has a copy. A film that screened but has no legal access is "unavailable".

About this register

The register is independent and is not affiliated with any studio, archive, festival or regulator.

It pays special attention to shorts and festival titles. A few found films stay because the register also tracks how films come back, through an archive print or a legal edition released years after a ban.

Each record starts with a documented screening, production or regulatory fact. Availability is checked against legal streaming, VOD, home video and archive catalogs, and the check date is recorded.

The 8,000+ figure is an archive estimate for films shot on film stock from 1919 to 2012. It is not a title list. The hard-to-find index starts from 2,034 Wikidata titles, screened against JustWatch Philippines on 1 November 2026. The estimate comes from The Philippine Star, 17 August 2026, citing Leo Katigbak of ABS-CBN Film Archives.

This is desk research, not an archive visit. Catalogs and region locks change, so a negative finding is not proof that no copy exists. No unauthorized full-film links are included. A correction needs a source and a changelog entry.
